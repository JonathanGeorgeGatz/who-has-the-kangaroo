Enclosure Check-Out Studio

A QR-driven check-out and check-in system for your animals. Staff scan a poster, tap twice, and the master dashboard always knows who is out, with whom, and for exactly how long.

How it flows

📱 Scan the OUT poster
Greeted by name · tap an available animal · outing starts
→
📊 The Sheet logs everything
Who, which animal, timestamp, running timer on the dashboard
→
🏡 Scan the BACK poster
Their animal is pre-selected · checklist + comments · tucked in

The two posters are generated for you, print-ready. Everything lives in your own Google account — no accounts to buy, nothing to install on the iPads.

Why not plain Google Forms?

The three features you asked for are exactly the ones a static Google Form cannot do. An Apps Script web app is Google's own free upgrade path: it opens from a QR code like a form, writes to the same spreadsheet — and it can think.

Remembers the person's deviceImpossibleGreets them by name, "Not you?" to switch

The four pages

…/exec?page=out

Taking a friend out?

Name chips (pre-selected on a known device), animals grouped by species — Chinchillas, Hedgehogs, Sugar Gliders, Lemurs… — with already-out animals hidden, one big Start button.

…/exec?page=in

Welcome back

Recognizes the person, pre-selects their animal, activity checklist (pee, poop, zoomies…), overlap-aware playmate picker, comments, done.

…/exec?page=board

Interactive dashboard

Live view with second-by-second timers (green < 1 h, amber, red past 2 h), plus tap-through Animals, Staff, and searchable History views — every animal and person opens a full detail card with stats and outing history. Refreshes itself.

…/exec?page=qr

QR posters

The two posters, styled and print-ready with generated QR codes. Print, laminate, mount by the enclosures.

The spreadsheet

  • Dashboard — stat tiles (out now · today · this month · this year · average outing), a live Out Right Now table with elapsed times, a Staff Spotlight dropdown that shows any person's complete outing history with dates, durations and comments, and monthly leaderboards for handlers and animals.
  • Log — one row per outing. Open outings glow gold until the animal is back.
  • Animals — the roster. Status, who has them, and since when fill themselves in.
  • People — staff & volunteers. Untick Active to hide someone from the forms.
  • Read Me — crib notes for whoever runs the system day to day.

How it was set up (for reference — already done)

  1. Create the script

    Signed into the organization's Google account, go to script.new. Replace the default Code.gs with the project's Code.gs, then add the five HTML files (＋ ▸ HTML): Styles, CheckOut, CheckIn, Board, QR — names must match exactly.

  2. Run setup() once

    Select the setup function and press Run; authorize when asked. It builds and styles the whole spreadsheet and prints its URL in the execution log.

  3. Deploy the web app

    Deploy ▸ New deployment ▸ Web app → Execute as: Me · Who has access: Anyone with the link. Copy the web app URL.

  4. Print the posters

    Open …/exec?page=qr, hit Print, laminate the two posters, and mount them where animals come and go.

  5. Two one-time settings

    In the spreadsheet: File ▸ Settings → set your time zone, and Calculation → "On change and every minute" so the dashboard's elapsed column ticks. Set the same time zone in the script's Project Settings.

  6. Load the real roster

    Replace the sample animals and people with yours, and open …/exec?page=board on the wall iPad. You're live.

Honest limits

"Device memory" is per-browser storage: a personal phone will always be greeted correctly, while a shared iPad shows the previous user's name with a one-tap Not you? switch. That is the true ceiling of device recognition without individual Google logins — anything more would mean making everyone sign in.

Re-running setup() later is safe: it rebuilds structure and styling but never touches logged data. If someone forgets to check in, just type the real Time In and duration into their row on the Log tab.
